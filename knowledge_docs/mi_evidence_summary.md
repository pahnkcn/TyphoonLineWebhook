# Motivational Interviewing Evidence Summary

## Purpose

This file gives a concise evidence-oriented summary of what motivational interviewing is good for in routine healthcare and substance-use counseling.

## Aliases and Retrieval Terms

- motivational interviewing evidence
- MI evidence
- brief intervention evidence
- counseling evidence
- evidence summary
- หลักฐาน MI
- งานวิจัย MI

## Core Evidence-Based Takeaways

- Motivational interviewing is most useful for **engagement**, **ambivalence**, and **readiness for change**.
- It tends to work best when it is:
  - empathic
  - collaborative
  - focused on the person’s own reasons for change
  - brief and well targeted
- MI is often used as part of a broader treatment plan rather than as a standalone cure-all.

## Where MI Helps Most

### Substance Use and Addiction

MI is useful for:

- reducing resistance to treatment discussions
- increasing willingness to consider change
- improving follow-through on treatment referrals
- strengthening motivation after relapse or treatment dropout

### General Healthcare

MI can support behavior change for:

- medication adherence
- chronic disease self-management
- alcohol and substance use screening follow-up
- smoking cessation
- lifestyle behavior change when ambivalence is present

## What MI Does Well

- improves engagement in difficult conversations
- reduces confrontation and shame
- helps clarify values and goals
- supports movement from uncertainty toward action
- fits brief visits, chat-based support, and early-stage counseling

## Limits of MI

MI is not enough by itself when the main need is:

- emergency medical care
- overdose response
- severe withdrawal management
- acute psychosis or severe agitation
- immediate suicide prevention steps

In those situations, use direct safety guidance first.

## Best-Practice Clinical Uses

MI is especially appropriate when someone says:

- I know this is a problem but I am not ready.
- I want to stop, but part of me does not.
- I am tired of disappointing people.
- I do not believe I can do it.
- I keep relapsing and feel hopeless.

## Practical Implication for This Chatbot

The chatbot should use MI to:

- reflect emotion and meaning
- avoid shame and argument
- evoke change talk
- support autonomy
- help build a small next step
- shift into direct emergency advice when safety risks are high
