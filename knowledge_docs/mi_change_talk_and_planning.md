# MI Change Talk and Planning

## Purpose

This guide focuses on **change talk**, **sustain talk**, **ambivalence**, readiness/confidence work, and turning motivation into a realistic plan.

## Definitions

### Change Talk

Statements that move toward change.

Examples:

- I do not want this to control me anymore.
- I miss who I used to be.
- I think I need help.
- อยากลดลงก่อน เพราะเริ่มกระทบงานแล้ว
- ไม่อยากให้แม่ต้องกังวลแบบนี้ต่อ

### Sustain Talk

Statements that support staying the same.

Examples:

- It is the only thing that helps me calm down.
- I am not ready.
- My friends all use it.
- ถ้าเลิกตอนนี้ไม่รู้จะรับมือความเครียดยังไง
- กลัวอาการถอนมาก

### Ambivalence

Ambivalence means both sides are present at once: wanting relief from the substance and also wanting relief from the consequences.

## DARN-CAT Framework

### Preparatory Change Talk: DARN

- Desire: I want to stop using every day.
- Ability: Maybe I could cut down on weekdays.
- Reasons: I am spending too much money and hiding things.
- Need: I need to do something before this gets worse.

### Mobilizing Change Talk: CAT

- Commitment: I am going to call the clinic tomorrow.
- Activation: I am ready to try a plan this week.
- Taking steps: I already threw away my dealer’s number.

Mobilizing language is stronger and usually predicts action better than vague interest alone.

## How to Respond to Change Talk

When change talk appears:

- reflect it
- ask for elaboration
- reinforce values and strengths
- ask what step would fit now

Examples:

- You really do not want this to keep stealing your time.
- What tells you now might be the right time to try something different?
- คุณเริ่มเห็นชัดขึ้นว่าเรื่องนี้กระทบคนที่คุณรัก
- ถ้าจะขยับไปข้างหน้าเพียงนิดเดียว คุณอยากเริ่มตรงไหน

## How to Respond to Sustain Talk

Do not argue against sustain talk. Reflect it carefully and keep curiosity.

Examples:

- Part of you feels the substance is still doing an important job for you.
- You are worried that stopping now could leave you overwhelmed.
- ตอนนี้มันยังดูเหมือนเป็นวิธีที่เร็วที่สุดในการเอาตัวรอด

Then explore gently:

- What do you like about it?
- And what has become harder because of it?
- มีด้านไหนที่ช่วยคุณอยู่จริง และมีด้านไหนที่เริ่มทำให้ชีวิตหนักขึ้น

## Developing Discrepancy

Discrepancy means noticing the gap between:

- current behavior
- personal values
- future goals

Helpful prompts:

- How does your current use fit with the parent/partner/student/worker you want to be?
- What has become more important to you lately?
- ถ้าสถานการณ์ยังเหมือนเดิมอีก 3 เดือน คุณกังวลเรื่องไหนที่สุด

## Readiness and Confidence Rulers

### Readiness Ruler

Ask:

- On a scale from 0 to 10, how ready are you to make a change?
- ตอนนี้ความพร้อมในการเปลี่ยนอยู่ที่กี่คะแนนจาก 0 ถึง 10

Follow-up questions:

- Why are you at a 4 and not a 1?
- What would help move you from 4 to 5?

### Confidence Ruler

Ask:

- On a scale from 0 to 10, how confident are you that you can do this step?
- ความมั่นใจว่าทำได้อยู่ที่กี่คะแนนจาก 0 ถึง 10

Follow-up questions:

- What helped you get that high already?
- What would make the plan easier or safer?

## Turning Motivation into a Plan

A good plan is:

- specific
- small enough to start now
- connected to the person’s own reason
- realistic about triggers and obstacles

### Simple Planning Template

- Goal for the next 24 hours or 7 days
- Trigger I expect
- Coping action I will try
- Support person or service I can contact
- Safety step if cravings or distress rise

### Example

- Goal: No heroin use tonight
- Trigger: loneliness after 9 PM
- Coping action: shower, leave room, call cousin, drink water, 10-minute urge surfing
- Support: one trusted person and treatment hotline
- Safety: call emergency services if overdose risk or severe withdrawal signs appear

## If the Person Is Not Ready

If readiness is low, do not force a commitment.

Use these goals instead:

- build alliance
- reduce shame
- clarify values
- notice patterns
- identify one safer choice

Examples:

- Even if you are not ready to stop, what would help you stay safer this week?
- ถ้ายังไม่อยากเลิกตอนนี้ อย่างน้อยอะไรจะช่วยให้เสี่ยงน้อยลงบ้าง
